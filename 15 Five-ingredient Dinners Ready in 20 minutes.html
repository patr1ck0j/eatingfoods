15 Five-Ingredient Dinner Recipes

1. Honey Garlic Chicken & Rice

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Sweet, sticky, and better than takeout.

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs
  • Soy sauce
  • Honey
  • Garlic
  • Rice

How to prepare

  1. Cook rice according to package.
  2. Sear chicken 5 min per side in a skillet.
  3. Add soy sauce, honey, and minced garlic. Simmer 5 min until glazed.
  4. Serve over rice.
Pro tip: Swap chicken for tofu for a vegetarian version.

2. Caprese Chicken Skillet

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: One pan, juicy chicken, and fresh mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast
  • Tomatoes
  • Mozzarella
  • Basil
  • Balsamic glaze

How to prepare

  1. Sear chicken 6 min per side.
  2. Add sliced tomatoes and cook 2 min.
  3. Top with mozzarella, cover 2 min to melt.
  4. Garnish with basil and balsamic glaze.

3. BBQ Chicken Quesadillas

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 15 min

Why you'll love it: Crispy, cheesy, and kid-approved.

Ingredients

  • Cooked chicken
  • BBQ sauce
  • Cheddar
  • Tortillas
  • Red onion

How to prepare

  1. Toss chicken with BBQ sauce and onion.
  2. Fill tortilla with chicken, cheese. Fold.
  3. Cook 3 min per side until crispy.
  4. Slice and serve.

4. Pesto Chicken & Pasta

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Creamy pesto without the cream.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Pasta
  • Pesto
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Parmesan

How to prepare

  1. Boil pasta, reserve 1/4 cup water.
  2. Sauté chicken 6-7 min.
  3. Toss pasta with pesto, pasta water, chicken, tomatoes.
  4. Top with parmesan.

5. 15-Minute Garlic Butter Salmon & Broccoli

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 15 min

Why you'll love it: Healthy, fast, and zero cleanup.

Ingredients

  • Salmon fillets
  • Broccoli florets
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Lemon

How to prepare

  1. Steam broccoli 4 min.
  2. Sear salmon 4 min per side in butter and garlic.
  3. Squeeze lemon over both.
  4. Serve immediately.

6. Lemon Garlic Shrimp Pasta

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Bright and restaurant-style at home.

Ingredients

  • Shrimp
  • Pasta
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Lemon

How to prepare

  1. Cook pasta, reserve water.
  2. Sauté garlic in butter, add shrimp 3 min per side.
  3. Toss with pasta, lemon juice, and pasta water.
  4. Finish with zest.

7. Shrimp Taco Bowls

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 15 min

Why you'll love it: Takeout vibes in 15 minutes.

Ingredients

  • Shrimp
  • Taco seasoning
  • Rice
  • Black beans
  • Lime

How to prepare

  1. Heat rice and beans.
  2. Season and sear shrimp 2 min per side.
  3. Build bowls with rice, beans, shrimp.
  4. Squeeze lime on top.

8. Beef Stir Fry & Bell Peppers

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Faster than delivery.

Ingredients

  • Beef strips
  • Bell peppers
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic
  • Rice noodles

How to prepare

  1. Soak noodles in hot water.
  2. Stir fry beef and garlic 4 min.
  3. Add peppers and soy sauce. Cook 3 min.
  4. Toss with noodles.

9. Spicy Sausage & Peppers

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Sheet pan dinner with no dishes.

Ingredients

  • Italian sausage
  • Bell peppers
  • Onion
  • Olive oil
  • Hoagie rolls

How to prepare

  1. Toss sausage, peppers, onion with oil.
  2. Roast at 425F for 15 min.
  3. Serve in hoagie rolls.
  4. Drizzle with pan juices.

10. Turkey Taco Skillet

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: One pan, big flavor.

Ingredients

  • Ground turkey
  • Taco seasoning
  • Black beans
  • Corn
  • Cheese

How to prepare

  1. Brown turkey 6 min.
  2. Add seasoning, beans, corn. Cook 5 min.
  3. Top with cheese, cover to melt.
  4. Serve with chips.

11. Chickpea Curry in 20 Minutes

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Creamy, vegan, and freezer-friendly.

Ingredients

  • Canned chickpeas
  • Coconut milk
  • Curry paste
  • Spinach
  • Rice

How to prepare

  1. Start rice.
  2. Heat curry paste, add coconut milk and chickpeas. Simmer 10 min.
  3. Stir in spinach.
  4. Serve over rice.

12. Teriyaki Tofu & Broccoli

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Crispy tofu without frying.

Ingredients

  • Tofu
  • Broccoli
  • Teriyaki sauce
  • Rice
  • Sesame oil

How to prepare

  1. Pan fry tofu cubes in sesame oil 5 min.
  2. Add broccoli, steam 4 min.
  3. Pour teriyaki sauce and toss.
  4. Serve over rice.

13. Tomato Basil Pasta

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Pantry dinner that feels fancy.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Canned tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Parmesan
  • Basil

How to prepare

  1. Cook pasta, reserve water.
  2. Sauté garlic, add tomatoes, simmer 8 min.
  3. Toss pasta in sauce.
  4. Top with basil and parmesan.

14. Egg Fried Rice

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 15 min

Why you'll love it: Uses leftover rice perfectly.

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Eggs
  • Soy sauce
  • Peas
  • Green onion

How to prepare

  1. Scramble eggs, remove.
  2. Stir fry rice, peas, soy sauce 3 min.
  3. Add eggs back in.
  4. Top with green onion.

15. Mediterranean Chickpea Salad Wrap

Prep: 10 min | Cook: 0 min | Total: 10 min

Why you'll love it: No cooking required.

Ingredients

  • Chickpeas
  • Cucumber
  • Feta
  • Tzatziki
  • Tortillas

How to prepare

  1. Rinse chickpeas, dice cucumber.
  2. Mix with feta and tzatziki.
  3. Spoon into tortillas.
  4. Wrap and serve.

FAQs

What counts as an ingredient?

Salt, pepper, and cooking oil are considered pantry staples and don't count toward your 5.

Can I meal prep these?

Yes! Most of these keep 3-4 days in the fridge. Check out our High Protein Meal Prep Recipes.

What if I only have 15 minutes?

Skip the rice and serve proteins over greens or in tortillas to save 5 minutes.
