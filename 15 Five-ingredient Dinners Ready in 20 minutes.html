Chicken Recipes

1. Honey Garlic Chicken & Rice

Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 20 min

Why you'll love it: Sweet, sticky, and better than takeout.

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs
  • Soy sauce
  • Honey
  • Garlic
  • Rice

Instructions

  1. Cook rice according to package.
  2. Sear chicken 5 min per side.
  3. Add soy sauce, honey, garlic. Simmer 5 min until glazed.
Pro tip: Swap chicken for tofu for a vegetarian version.

FAQs

What counts as an ingredient?

Salt, pepper, and cooking oil are considered pantry staples and don't count toward your 5.

Can I meal prep these?

Yes! Most of these keep 3-4 days in the fridge. Check out our High Protein Meal Prep Recipes.

What if I only have 15 minutes?

Skip the rice and serve proteins over greens or in tortillas to save 5 minutes.
