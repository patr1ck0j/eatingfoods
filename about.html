About eatingfoods

Hi! I'm Ogonna. I created eatingfoods.com to help busy people get dinner on the table fast without sacrificing flavor.

Our Mission: Real food, real fast. 5 ingredients or less. 20-30 minutes max. No fancy equipment, no 2-hour recipes.

Why I Started This Blog

After too many nights of takeout and complicated recipes with 20 ingredients, I realized busy people need something different. Every recipe here is tested in my own kitchen in Port Harcourt with real time limits. If it takes longer than 30 minutes, it doesn't make the cut.

What You'll Find Here

  • 5-Ingredient Dinners: Simple meals with pantry staples
  • 20-Minute Meals: Fast dinners for weeknights
  • Low Carb & Keto Options: Under 10g net carbs per serving
  • Family Friendly: Recipes everyone will actually eat

Recipe Promise

All recipes are tested 3 times before publishing. We use ingredients you can find at any grocery store, and we clearly list prep time, cook time, and net carbs for low carb meals.
