How to Meal Prep for the Week in 2 Hours

By Ogonna | Updated: Oct 31, 2026

You can prepare 5 days of high-protein lunches and dinners in just 2 hours. This is the exact system I use every Sunday.

Meal prepping doesn’t have to take your entire weekend. With the right plan, you can cook once and eat well all week. Here’s the simple 2-hour method.

The 2-Hour Meal Prep System

  1. Plan (10 minutes): Choose 2–3 proteins, 2 vegetables, and 1–2 carb sources. Pick from our high protein meal prep recipes.
  2. Shop (20 minutes): Stick to the list. No extras.
  3. Prep ingredients (20 minutes): Chop vegetables, measure spices, start rice or quinoa.
  4. Cook proteins (40 minutes): Bake chicken, cook ground turkey, or grill in batches.
  5. Assemble & store (30 minutes): Portion into containers while things are still warm.

What to Cook in Batches

  • Chicken breast or thighs
  • Ground turkey or lean beef
  • Roasted vegetables (broccoli, peppers, zucchini)
  • Rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice

Storage Tips

Most meals last 4–5 days in the fridge. Freeze anything you won’t eat by day 4. Label containers with the date.
